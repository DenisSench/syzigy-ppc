Why one hook is the real fatigue risk

A single hook carries most of active spend, so when it tires it drags the whole account down at once — that's what happened to Launch (CTR 1.66%). New creators reading the same script won't fix it; you need genuinely new hooks, 2–3 live per ad set so Meta can rotate.
